EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS REPORT
Books Dataset

============================================================

1. INTRODUCTION
   ============================================================

This report presents the results of Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) performed on a dataset containing 1,000 books.

The analysis focuses on understanding the dataset structure, checking data quality, examining book prices and ratings, identifying patterns, detecting outliers, and studying the relationship between book price and rating.

============================================================

2. DATASET OVERVIEW
   ============================================================

Number of records: 1,000

Number of variables: 5

Variables:

* title
* price_gbp
* rating
* availability
* url

The dataset contains book titles, prices in British pounds, ratings from 1 to 5, availability information, and the URL of each book.

============================================================

3. DATA QUALITY
   ============================================================

Missing values:

* title: 0
* price_gbp: 0
* rating: 0
* availability: 0
* url: 0

Duplicate rows: 0

All 1,000 books are listed as "In stock".

Therefore, no missing values or duplicate rows were identified in the dataset.

============================================================

4. PRICE ANALYSIS
   ============================================================

Average price: £35.07

Median price: £35.98

Minimum price: £10.00

Maximum price: £59.99

The prices range from £10.00 to £59.99. The mean and median prices are relatively close, indicating that the central tendency of the price distribution is around £35.

============================================================

5. RATING ANALYSIS
   ============================================================

Rating distribution:

1 star: 226 books
2 stars: 196 books
3 stars: 203 books
4 stars: 179 books
5 stars: 196 books

The 1-star rating is the most frequent rating, while the 4-star rating is the least frequent.

============================================================

6. PRICE AND RATING RELATIONSHIP
   ============================================================

The correlation between price and rating is:

0.028166

This value is very close to zero.

Therefore, there is an extremely weak positive linear relationship between book price and rating in this dataset.

The result suggests that book price does not have a meaningful linear relationship with rating within the analyzed dataset.

============================================================

7. OUTLIER ANALYSIS
   ============================================================

The Interquartile Range (IQR) method was used to detect potential price outliers.

Lower limit: -£15.92

Upper limit: £85.48

Number of detected price outliers: 0

No book prices were classified as outliers using the 1.5 × IQR rule.

============================================================

   * Shows the frequency of each book rating.

3. Price by Rating

   * Compares book price distributions across ratings.

These visualizations support the statistical findings and provide a visual understanding of the dataset.

============================================================

9. KEY FINDINGS
   ============================================================

10. The dataset contains 1,000 books and 5 variables.

11. No missing values were found.

12. No duplicate records were found.

13. The average book price is £35.07.

14. The median book price is £35.98.

15. Book prices range from £10.00 to £59.99.

16. The 1-star rating is the most common rating.

17. All books are listed as being in stock.

18. No price outliers were detected using the IQR method.

19. The correlation between price and rating is approximately 0.0282, indicating almost no linear relationship.

============================================================

10. CONCLUSION
    ============================================================

The exploratory data analysis successfully examined the structure, quality, distribution, and relationships within the books dataset.

The dataset was found to be complete and free from duplicate records. Book prices showed a range from £10.00 to £59.99, with an average price of £35.07.

The rating distribution was relatively spread across the five rating categories, with 1-star books being the most frequent.

The correlation analysis showed that price and rating have almost no linear relationship in this dataset. Therefore, higher-priced books cannot be assumed to have higher ratings based on this dataset.

Overall, the EDA provided useful insights into the dataset and demonstrated how statistical analysis and visualization can be used to understand data before applying further analytical techniques.
